### Task 1: `battery_current_a` follows the model — the ESC rating stops clamping (#164)

The chain, named end to end (the issue's first done-when): `EscMotor::step` clamps each motor's winding current to ±`current_limit_a` (`crates/ofs-electrical/src/esc_motor.rs:87`, `current = ((applied_v − ke·ω)/R).clamp(-limit, limit) × fault scale`) and publishes `esc.{i}.bus_current = duty × current` (:98); the battery sums the four (`crates/ofs-electrical/src/battery.rs:117`) into `battery.current_a`; `State.battery_current_a` reads that signal (`crates/ofs-sim/src/vehicle.rs:1707`, handle at :441); Betaflight sees the same figure as its ESC-sensor sum (`crates/ofs-fc/src/sitl/esc_telemetry.rs:87` sends `battery.current_a / motors` per frame, which the firmware adds back up — the roadmap ruling at `docs/roadmap.md:189-191` stands either way). At full throttle every motor sits on the clamp, so the state reads exactly 4 × 45 A = 180 A on the 5F (`library/escs/opendrone-4in1-esc.toml`, `current_limit_a = 45.0`). The clamp is the modelling artifact; per the ruling it goes, and the rating becomes data.

### Task 2: A measured magnetometer into Betaflight SITL — the reserved mag block (#165)

Today the compass stops at the sim: `Mag` publishes `mag.field_frd_ut` and `mag.heading_rad` when the quad carries a part (`ofs-sim/src/vehicle.rs:1013-1026`; signal names at `ofs-core/src/names.rs:57-59`), but the SITL state datagram has no field for it, so Betaflight synthesises its own compass from the attitude quaternion and a fixed field — 4096 counts, 60° inclination, 2° east (`docs/research/sitl-interface.md` §3, the `imu_orientation_quat` row) — and noise, hard iron, soft iron and the world's declination never reach the firmware. The compass spec already scoped this out ("a new reserved block like the eRPM block 250 would do it", `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-compass-design.md`); this task does it.
